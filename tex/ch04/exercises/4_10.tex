We have for $p = 1$ that
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        \log{\biggl(\frac{\text{Pr}(Y = k \vert X = x)}{\text{Pr}(Y = K \vert X = x)}\biggr)}
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k f_k(x)}{\pi_K f_K(x)}\biggr)} \\
        &= \log{\Biggl(\frac{
            \pi_k \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma_k} \exp{\bigl(-\frac{1}{2\sigma_k^2} {(x - \mu_k)}^2\bigr)}
        }{
            \pi_K \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma_K} \exp{\bigl(-\frac{1}{2\sigma_K^2} {(x - \mu_K)}^2\bigr)}
        }\Biggr)} \\
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\biggr)} - \log{\biggl(\frac{\sigma_k}{\sigma_K}\biggr)}
            - \frac{1}{2\sigma_k^2} {(x - \mu_k)}^2 \\
        &\qquad + \frac{1}{2\sigma_K^2} {(x - \mu_K)}^2.
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
If we assume that the mean $u_k$ is class-specific but the variance is shared between 
all classes, as with the shared covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma}$, then we have
$\sigma_k = \sigma_K$ for all $k = 1, \ldots, K$, so that we have
\begin{equation*}
    \begin{split}
        \log{\biggl(\frac{\text{Pr}(Y = k \vert X = x)}{\text{Pr}(Y = K \vert X = x)}\biggr)}
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\biggr)} - \log{\biggl(\frac{\sigma_K}{\sigma_K}\biggr)}
            - \frac{1}{2\sigma_K^2} {(x - \mu_k)}^2 \\
        &\qquad + \frac{1}{2\sigma_K^2} {(x - \mu_K)}^2 \\
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\biggr)} - \log{1} - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} {(x - \mu_k)}^2 \\
        &\qquad + \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} {(x - \mu_K)}^2 \\
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\biggr)} + \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (x^2 - 2\mu_k x - \mu_k^2 \\
        &\qquad - x^2 + 2\mu_K x + \mu_K^2) \\
        &= \log{\biggl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\biggr)} + \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (\mu_K^2 - \mu_k^2)
        + \frac{1}{\sigma^2} (\mu_K - \mu_k) x \\
        &= a_k + b_{kj}x_j,
    \end{split}
\end{equation*}
where $a_k = \log{\Bigl(\frac{\pi_k}{\pi_K}\Bigr)} + \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} (\mu_K^2 - \mu_k^2)$ and
$b_{kj} = \frac{1}{\sigma^2} (\mu_K - \mu_k)$, with $j = 1$.
